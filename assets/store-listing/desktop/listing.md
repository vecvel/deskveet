## Name

Deskveet

## Subtitle (30 characters)

Clean caches, keep control

## Short description (one sentence)

A small, fast and private disk cleaner that shows where your space went and only cleans what is safe to clean.

## Description

Deskveet shows where your disk space went and cleans what is safe to clean.

Most full disks are full of things nobody chose to keep: build folders, package caches, browser caches, logs and
the downloads tools leave behind. Deskveet knows where these live, measures them, and tells you what each one is
before anything is touched.

EVERY RULE EXPLAINS ITSELF
Built-in rules cover developer tools, package managers, browsers and app caches. Each one says what happens if you
delete its items and how they come back, so you decide with the facts in front of you.

YOU SEE IT FIRST
Nothing is cleaned until you have seen the list and confirmed it. Items move to the bin, so you can put them back,
and an undo log keeps track of what moved where. Running browsers and open files are left alone.

SEE WHERE YOUR SPACE WENT
A fast scan of any folder, with sizes per folder and a treemap you can scroll through so even small folders get a
label.

LARGE FILES AND DUPLICATES
Find files over a size you pick that have not changed in months, and files with identical contents anywhere in a
folder. Duplicates are compared by content, and the last copy of a file is never offered.

APPS, LEFTOVERS AND STARTUP
Remove apps, find what apps you already removed left behind, and see what starts when you log in.

YOUR OWN RULES
Add a rule for anything you clear out by hand, such as old disk images in Downloads, with a live preview of what it
matches.

TRACKING AND SCHEDULES
Size history per rule, an alert when something grows past a size you pick or the disk gets low, and optional daily
or weekly cleaning of the safe rules.

YOUR COMPUTER AT A GLANCE
Disks, memory, CPU per core and the busiest processes. From the tray you can clean safe caches, quit apps, keep the
computer awake, eject drives, and lock, sleep, restart or shut down.

SMALL AND PRIVATE
A few megabytes, around 30 MB of memory with its window closed, no accounts and no tracking. Its only connection is
a daily check on GitHub for updates, which you can turn off.

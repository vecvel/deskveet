# Code of conduct

Deskveet is a small project and we want it to stay a friendly place to ask questions, report problems and
suggest ideas. This page explains what we expect from everyone who takes part: in issues, discussions, pull
requests and anywhere else the project is represented.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. People come with different backgrounds, levels of experience and first languages.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude someone meant harm.
- Keep criticism about the work, not the person. Explain what you would change and why.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and say so when you got something wrong.
- Respect people's time. Search before opening an issue and include what is needed to reproduce a problem.
- Respect privacy. Never post someone else's personal information, and leave your own personal file names
  and paths out of screenshots and logs where you can.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, background, body or abilities.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Deliberately derailing discussions, trolling or repeatedly pushing a point after being asked to stop.
- Sharing private conversations or anyone's private information without their clear permission.
- Encouraging or helping with any of the above.

## Reporting

If something happens that makes you uncomfortable, please tell us. Use GitHub's private reporting on this
repository (the Security tab, then "Report a vulnerability", works for conduct reports too) or contact the
maintainers through https://github.com/vecvel. Reports are read by the maintainers only and handled in
confidence. We will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.

## What happens next

The maintainers will look at every report and decide on a fair response. Depending on what happened, that can
be a private conversation, a public or private warning, removal of comments or contributions, or a temporary or
permanent ban from the project. We will explain the decision to the people involved when we can.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and in public spaces when someone is representing the project.

This code of conduct takes its spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
